## Known Risks

- [RISK-001|recovery] `Recovery::Full` remains the largest semantic-risk area: replay can duplicate messages, topology replay can partially fail, and consumer tags may conflict after partitions. Server-named queue remap, default-exchange pending publish replay, caller-close finality, topology/consumer replay fail-closed behavior, duplicate-delivery caveat, and explicit consumer-tag replay are now covered. Status: active residual distributed-systems risk. Trust `{F:0.84,G:0.8,R:0.86}`.

- [RISK-002|consumer] Consumer backpressure first stalls the per-channel handler, not the entire connection reader. Whole-connection backpressure remains possible only if the blocked channel's frame inbox fills. Status: partially mitigated by docs correction and opt-in live harness; eventual saturation coverage remains active. Trust `{F:0.7,G:0.7,R:0.7}`.

- [RISK-003|confirms] The named deterministic publisher-confirm cleanup gaps are now covered: multiple ack/nack, returned-before-ack, timeout then late ack, close while blocked, unread async outcome, batch sequence registration, mode-aware replay-payload retention, and low-watermark progress tracking. Residual risk is real-broker rare ordering under load, not missing local falsifiers. Status: mitigated with residual live/perf risk. Trust `{F:0.86,G:0.7,R:0.9}`.

- [RISK-004|performance] Concrete performance targets are still not release guarantees, but the risk is lower now that `tools/perf_publish.cr` is checked in, direct writer optimizations have byte-equivalence witnesses, recovery-disabled confirm tracking no longer retains replay bodies, socket throughput defaults are configurable and old-client-aligned, and the harness can distinguish synthetic frame encoding from live multi-channel, shared-queue multi-connection, and separate-queue multi-connection publish throughput. Local numbers remain directional because broker/compiler/load variance is uncontrolled and `amqp-client.cr` parity is still from scratch/local harnesses, but paired release runs now show fire-and-forget parity/slight lead on this host. Status: partially mitigated by hot-path cleanup, batch API, checked-in witness harness, mode-aware confirm retention, socket tuning, attribution probes, unconfirmed-publish lock elision, and roadmap downgrade. Trust `{F:0.86,G:0.5,R:0.84}`.
